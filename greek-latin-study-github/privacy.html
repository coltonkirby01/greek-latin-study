Privacy

Privacy notice

If you use the site without signing in, study progress is stored only in your browser. If you sign in, the authentication provider and Supabase may process basic account information such as your email address, name, profile image, and account identifier. The study site stores your flashcard progress, including responses, difficulty ratings, review intervals, and review timestamps, so that your progress can sync across devices.

The site does not need payment information or sensitive personal information. Do not enter sensitive information into the study site.

You can remove study progress by using the reset controls for each deck. Account-level deletion should be handled through the site's Supabase project administrator.
