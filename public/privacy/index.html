Account and study-data privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Greek & Latin Study is an educational application for flashcards, adaptive review, and reading practice. This policy explains what information the application processes and why.

Information the application processes

  • Account information. When you create an account or use Google sign-in, Supabase Auth processes your email address, an account identifier, and basic profile information supplied by Google. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not stored or viewed by this application.
  • Study information. The application may store card responses, correctness and difficulty ratings, response times, mastery strength, scheduling dates, review history, deck progress, saved passages, playback preferences, timing metadata, and administrator-created deck content.
  • User-provided files and text. If you save a reading, import a deck or backup, or upload audio, the application processes that material to provide the feature you requested.
  • Guest-device information. Before sign-in, study progress and saved readings may be kept in your browser's local storage. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.
  • Operational information. GitHub Pages, Supabase, and your browser may process ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser type, request logs, and authentication-session data to host, secure, and operate the service.

Google account information

Google sign-in is used only to authenticate you and associate your private study data with your account. The application requests only basic identity scopes: OpenID, email address, and profile. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or other Google services, and it does not use Google account information for advertising.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, synchronize progress across devices, personalize adaptive review, restore study history, provide saved readings and audio, protect administrator functions, maintain security, and troubleshoot the application.

Service providers and disclosure

Supabase provides authentication, database, and private file-storage services. GitHub Pages hosts the public application files. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to operate the service. Personal information is not sold, rented, or used for third-party advertising. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, or secure the service.

Retention and control

Account-linked study information is retained while the account is active or as reasonably needed to operate and secure the service. Local guest information remains on the device until it is imported, replaced, or cleared. Users may export supported progress data from the Account page. For correction or deletion requests, contact the site administrator using the support contact shown during Google sign-in. Do not post private account information in a public GitHub issue.

Security

The application uses secure Supabase sessions and database row-level security so users can access only their own private progress and readings. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are used and secret credentials are not placed in public frontend code.

Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly solicit their personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied information may contact the site administrator to request its removal.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

General project information is available in the public GitHub repository. For account-specific or privacy requests, use the site administrator's support contact displayed by the Google sign-in consent screen rather than posting private details publicly.
